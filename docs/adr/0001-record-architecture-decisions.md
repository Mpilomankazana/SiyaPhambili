# 1. Record Architecture Decisions

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-09-06

## Context
We need to clearly record the architectural and technical decisions made for the SiyaPhambili platform. This ensures all team members understand the reasoning behind our technical choices, preventing duplicated discussions and keeping development aligned during the fast-paced 48-hour sprint.

## Decision
We will use Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) to document significant architectural choices. These records will follow a standardized template and be stored chronologically in the `docs/adr/` directory of our repository.

## Consequences
* **Positive:** The team has a permanent, easily accessible log of why specific tools and frameworks were chosen.
* **Positive:** Clarifies our engineering approach to the adjudication panel, demonstrating a progression toward Technology Readiness Level 4.
* **Negative:** Requires a small time investment to write the records before implementing major system changes.
